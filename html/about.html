01 / ABOUT

Every home tells a story. We help you design yours.

Alvora is an interior design and decor studio dedicated to homes. We believe a house becomes a home when every room reflects the people who live in it.

We bring together space planning, thoughtful materials and warm, layered styling to create interiors that feel calm, personal and effortlessly elegant: spaces made to be lived in, not just looked at.

From the first sketch to the final styling touch, we guide you through every step, from concept and design to sourcing and installation, so your vision becomes a home you will love for years to come.
